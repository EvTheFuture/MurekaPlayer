Privacy Policy for Mureka Player

Last updated: 12 September 2026

Mureka Player is a free, open source browser extension that adds a music player to mureka.ai so you can browse and listen to the songs in your own Mureka library. It is developed by EvTheFuture (Magnus Sandin) and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Mureka.

Summary

The extension collects nothing. It has no servers, no analytics, no telemetry and no advertising. No personal data is sent to the developer or to any third party. Everything it stores stays in your own browser, on your own device.

Data the extension does not collect

  • No account details, passwords or authentication tokens are read, stored or transmitted.
  • No browsing history, no page content from other sites, and no data from any site other than mureka.ai.
  • No analytics, usage statistics, crash reports, device identifiers or advertising identifiers.
  • Nothing is sent to the developer. The developer operates no server and receives no data of any kind.

Data stored on your device

To work offline and to avoid re-downloading the same content, the extension stores the following locally in your browser. None of it leaves your device, and all of it is removed if you uninstall the extension or clear the site data.

  • Your list of songs and their details, such as title, genre, duration and publish state.
  • Cached audio files, cover images and waveform data, so playback works offline.
  • Your own settings and preferences, such as the transport button layout, and any songs you have marked as instrumental.

Network requests

The extension communicates only with Mureka, using the login session already present in your browser. It does so on your behalf, in the same way the Mureka website itself does, and only while you are on mureka.ai.

  • mureka.ai is contacted to list the songs in your library and to read song details such as lyrics and play counts.
  • static-cos.mureka.ai is contacted to fetch the audio files and cover images for those songs.
  • When you play a song, a play is reported to Mureka, exactly as the Mureka website does. This can be turned off in the extension settings.
  • When you like or unlike a song in the player, that action is sent to Mureka so your library stays in step.

No other host is contacted. The extension contains all of its own code and never downloads or executes code from a remote source.

Downloads

The extension requests the downloads permission for one purpose only: when you choose to download your songs, it saves those mp3 files into a Mureka subfolder of your normal download directory. It does not read your existing downloads, and it never downloads anything without you asking.

Children

The extension is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, regardless of age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue at github.com/EvTheFuture/MurekaPlayer. The full source code of the extension is available there for inspection.
